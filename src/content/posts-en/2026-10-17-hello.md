## What Is This Site

MUR DOS LOG: a personal researcher's notebook. I write about AI application engineering, the enduring questions of consciousness and qualia, and the process of forging philosophy, methodology, and mathematics into tools.

## Two Generations of the Opening Animation

**Version one: a rainy night in the city → a CRT terminal → a girl under a lamp → MUR DOS LOG.** The atmosphere worked, but it was only **telling a story**—the rainy night, the terminal, and the girl were three beautiful set pieces placed side by side, with no necessary connection between them.

**The current version replaces that with self-reference.** Instead of telling a story, the entire opening lets the visuals **talk about themselves**:

1. **A quine runs in the terminal**: a program that prints its own source code verbatim without reading any files or accepting input. The code and its output are the same line of text—for the first time, the screen "speaks itself."
2. **Gödel numbering**: the program is encoded as an enormous integer. Code that talks about itself becomes arithmetic that talks about itself.
3. **Glitching into manga panels**: the numbers burst out of the terminal, and the image shatters into black-and-white manga panels.
4. **The manga girl discusses self-reference**: she writes G ↔ ¬Prov(⌜G⌝)—given a provability predicate, there exists a sentence G that says precisely, "I am not provable." Thus T ⊬ G and T ⊬ ¬G: within the system, this sentence can be neither proved nor refuted.
5. **Landing on the site name**: MUR DOS LOG.

Going from "a story on a rainy night" to "a machine talking about itself, and a girl talking about herself" is two iterations along the same trajectory. The opening animation itself is a sample of what this site aims to do: turn abstract, self-referential, mathematical ideas into things you can see.

The old city-night-rain set pieces have been taken entirely offline, and the related copy throughout the site has been replaced as well.

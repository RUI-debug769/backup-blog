Naval, the famous Silicon Valley investor, once said something very piercing yet utterly true: the only real test of intelligence is whether you can get what you want out of life. This statement completely overturns our traditional understanding of intelligence and redefines its core meaning for us. In our traditional understanding, intelligence is IQ—it's how many points you can score on an exam, whether you can get into a prestigious university, whether you can memorize more knowledge points, solve harder math problems, and get higher scores on standardized tests. Today's AI benchmarks are the same—just as we cannot quantify a person, the quantification of AI intelligence itself is also fitting. From the previous maximum model to the current suitable model.

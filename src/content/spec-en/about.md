# Murdo Klein

> Casting philosophical ideas, the methods of great minds, and mathematical tools into AI tools.

Behind every project stands a thinker: OCOS draws on Simon and Feynman, CRIA on the five-step research method, ai-brain-tools on the intersection of mathematics and brain science. The question I keep returning to is **consciousness and qualia**.

This is where I document the building process: design decisions, the pitfalls I hit, and the thoughts worth writing down. No standard answers—only perspectives.

## What I'm working on

- **AI application engineering**: turning cognitive architectures into open-source tools that ordinary people can actually use;
- **Consciousness and qualia**: long-term reading and experiments spanning philosophy, neuroscience, and BCI.
